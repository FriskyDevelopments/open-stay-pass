# HostCasa and Folios Visual Reference

## HostCasa.app

HostCasa uses a **cinematic night-hospitality** expression: near-black navy fields, subdued constellation-like texture, soft cyan/aqua light, occasional warm gold, a high-contrast serif wordmark treatment, narrow uppercase utility labels, and generous editorial whitespace. Its interaction tone is calm, private, and elevated. For Open Stay Pass, this language belongs on the guest-facing HostCasa arrival experience: a reassuring arrival card, property guidance, and a gently illuminated ConciergeAI conversation.

## Folios.works

Folios uses a **precise operating-ledger** expression: dark graphite fields, vivid emerald-green actions and status marks, dense but orderly record cards, a monospaced metadata layer, and a prominent modern sans headline. Its interaction tone is factual, clear, and operational. For Open Stay Pass, this language belongs on the Folios handoff and operator surfaces: evidence state, next owner, share status, credential state, and audit events.

## Shared application rule

Keep HostCasa and Folios recognizably distinct while sharing one layout system, bilingual controls, token status language, and accessible contrast. The public landing page may transition between the two visual worlds. Guest arrival pages should default to HostCasa; proof handoff and operator management should default to Folios. Avoid a generic third-brand dashboard.
