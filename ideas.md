# Open Stay Pass — Folios Compliance Visual Direction

> Historical Folios-specific direction. For current Open Stay Pass branding, follow [the Claude Design export](docs/brand/README.md) and [design_reference.md](design_reference.md). Folios colors and rounded panel measurements below must not be applied to the OSP community/docs or QR Studio surface.

The public experience is a **documentary credential system**, not a dark generic SaaS landing page and not a cinematic hotel advertisement. Use the Folios Compliance Brandbook V2 token contract with strict semantic discipline: **Ink `#102526`** for authority and operational fields; **Paper `#F2F0E9`** for fiscal evidence; **System `#5772C7`** for review and transfer; **Proof `#37CDE0`** for QR, provenance, and traceability; and **Signal `#C6F43D`** only for verified issued/valid decisions. Use **Error `#B3524A`** only for rejected or blocked action.

Typography should contrast Cormorant Garamond editorial decisions against Hanken Grotesk interface data. Geometry is squared and documentary: 4 px controls, 8 px panels, hairline Structure `#BAC0B7` rules, mono-derived metadata cadence, and no gratuitous glossy gradients. The visual protagonist is the credential record, its proof rail, and the controlled state transition—not a phone mockup or a generic dashboard.

Promo visuals must show a clear physical-to-record sequence: **Proof carrier (QR/NFC) → signed resolver → Paper record → Signal decision**. Never represent NFC as physical unlock, never use invented hotel UI or non-existent tax numbers, never put lock secrets in a public carrier, and never imply Google Wallet issuance while its signing credential remains blocked.
